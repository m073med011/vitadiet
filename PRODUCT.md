# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Vitadiet  serves two B2B audiences. The local audience is pharmacy owners, purchasing teams, authorized distributors, medical suppliers, and commercial partners in Saudi Arabia. The global audience is European manufacturers and supplement brands looking for an ethical, professional representative in the Saudi market.

## Product Purpose

The website presents Vitadiet  as a Saudi medical distribution company that operates exclusively on a B2B basis. It explains the company's exclusive European supplement agencies, SFDA-led compliance stance, scientific product selection process, distribution readiness, and ethical representation model. The site should make it clear that Vitadiet  does not sell directly to consumers.

## Brand Personality

Refined, scientific, pure, trustworthy, and calm. The voice should be modern Arabic first, with equally polished English, and should sound professional without being cold. It should avoid hype and instead build confidence through facts, documentation, quality, safety, and responsibility.

## Anti-references

Avoid ecommerce wellness-shop language, direct-to-consumer buying flows, influencer testimonials, exaggerated health claims, gym-supplement hype, fake partner logos, aggressive sales urgency, and generic medical stock cliches. The brand should not feel like a retail supplement store.

## Design Principles

1. Make purity tangible through restraint, white space, and soft lotus-inspired forms.
2. Lead with science, safety, and regulatory trust before commercial persuasion.
3. Speak clearly to both Saudi B2B partners and European brand principals.
4. Use premium details sparingly: gold is an accent, not decoration everywhere.
5. Show the full partner journey: selection, compliance, packaging, distribution, documents, and contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain bilingual English and Arabic support with correct LTR and RTL behavior. Use readable contrast, calm motion, plain language, and scannable sections for procurement and regulatory audiences. Do not rely on color alone for meaning.
