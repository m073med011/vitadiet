# Design

## Visual Theme

Vitadiet  uses a clean premium medical-distribution identity: deep navy for trust, white and cool neutrals for purity, and restrained gold for value and certification. The site should feel like a refined company profile and partner dossier, not a retail shop.

## Typography

- Arabic: Tajawal, modern and readable for formal Arabic content.
- English: Poppins, clear and present without feeling overly corporate.
- Headings are confident, spacious, and concise.
- Body copy is direct, factual, and capped to comfortable reading widths.

## Colors

- Primary navy: `#1B3861`
- Premium gold: `#D4AF37`, used at roughly 5-10 percent of the visual surface.
- Light surface: warm-white tinted toward blue.
- Muted surface: very light blue-gray.
- Text: deep navy and softened blue-gray.

## Components

- Header: calm, text-led brand mark with a small petal symbol.
- Hero: first screen should communicate Vitadiet , B2B-only distribution, European agencies, and Saudi market access.
- Petal system: six petal cards for the USP section.
- Timelines: soft path from Europe to Saudi Arabia and a four-step partner journey.
- Product cards: large white space, real product imagery, and hover overlays for ingredients, warnings, and compliance notes.
- Download center: compact document tiles for brochures, certificates, and product files.
- FAQ: inline accordion-style content, not modal-first.

## Imagery

Use realistic supplement, laboratory, packaging, and pharmacy/distribution imagery. Avoid overprocessed wellness lifestyle photography. Lotus or white-flower forms may appear as low-opacity patterns, dividers, or petal structures, but should not overwhelm the content.

## Motion

Use subtle hover and scroll-feel transitions only. Motion should support clarity, not entertain. Buttons and cards may lift slightly; no distracting effects.
